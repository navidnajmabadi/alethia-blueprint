Token cost is nearly free. Human time is not.

Alethia is an AI consultant, but every case is checked by Navid and Morteza before it is fully trusted. That single fact shapes what the system charges, what it gives away, and where the coin economy stops. The rest of this sheet follows from it.

01

Business model overview

Four blocks. What runs now, what comes later, how clients bring clients, and how money comes in.

Key Activities

Year 1
  • Run diagnostics and maintain the P1 to P12 profile logic
  • Build and expand the AI Consulting Brain: playbooks per profile
  • Human-in-the-loop review by Navid and Morteza

    Their corrections feed back into the playbooks. That is how the system learns.

  • OP3 generation and weekly mentoring operation
  • Material library curation

  • Vertical-specific playbook packs
  • Third-party solution provider marketplace
  • White label or API licensing

The engagement loop

Every hit target sends the client back to the start with a new weakness to find.

  1. 1Diagnose

    EarnFinishing the twelve questions

    SpendRe-running a diagnosis early

  2. 2Select solution

    EarnNothing here, on purpose

    SpendUnlocking a second-ranked option

  3. 3Implement

    EarnProof uploaded on a task

    SpendRegenerating the one-page plan

  4. 4Track

    EarnA weekly check-in streak

    SpendAn extra mentor question between check-ins

05

Tools by level

A phased rollout. Each level is what the business needs to have running by then.

Year 1

Level 1, Entry

Mandatory
  • Diagnostic engine
  • Basic AI Consulting Brain covering top profiles
  • Template-based OP3 generator
  • Manual or single-integration KPI dashboard
  • Weekly mentoring via chat or email
  • Manual human review queue
  • Basic coin ledger

Years 2 to 3

Level 2, Scale

Needed to compete
  • Data integrations for automatic KPI pull
  • Automated referral program
  • Expanded material marketplace
  • Sampled review graduation logic
  • Mobile push
  • Tiered billing with coin top-ups
  • Early partner portal

Year 5 and beyond

Level 3, Mature

Mature
  • White label licensing platform
  • Predictive diagnosis from connected data
  • Competitive marketplace of third-party solution providers
  • Internal model tuned on aggregated case history
  • Full API ecosystem for embedding Alethia in other platforms
