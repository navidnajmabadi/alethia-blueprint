Value proposition and moat

What Alethia promises, and why it cannot be copied.

Alethia tells a small business exactly what is holding revenue back, then stays until it is fixed.

It diagnoses the business into one of twelve constraint profiles, then runs the engagement: a plan, KPI tracking, weekly AI mentoring and a tool library, paid for with Coin.

What a competitor can copy

  • The interface and screens
  • The look of a diagnostic report
  • A generic AI prompt

What they cannot

  • Two years of human-reviewed, corrected cases
  • A playbook library per profile, sharpened by each correction
  • Case history tied to real KPI movement

The moat is the playbook library, not the interface. Every human-reviewed case makes the diagnostic sharper. Any competitor can copy a UI. Nobody can copy two years of corrected cases.

The scale point

The scale point is the month Mid and High Coin revenue first covers fixed cost. Past it, serving one more Coin costs 0.4¢ and sells for 5.0¢, so each Coin earns about 12× its cost.
